    Limits

    Each route is one tech's day: leave the shop, farthest job first, work back, and end at the shop. AM or PM is assigned from the arrival time (before noon is AM). A job can't start after 4:00 PM, so on Auto the planner adds routes when a day won't fit. Leave "Number of routes" blank for the fewest.

    Routes

    Add jobs on the left and press Optimize. You'll get each route in driving order with arrival times, a map, and the drive time between every pair of jobs.

    Drive times are average road times plus your traffic buffer, not live traffic.
